# The AI Compass

Books, movies, AI builders and companies plotted on two axes: how they see human nature, and what they expect from AI. A 17-statement quiz places you on the same map, scored on the same statements as every entry.

Everything is in `index.html`. Open it in a browser or visit the GitHub Pages site.

Every placement is our reading of the work or person. Book and movie quotes come from the works themselves. Quotes from people and companies link to the pages we checked them against in November 2026.
